Dyed privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Dyed is a puzzle game for Android and iOS made by Florian Kirchner (codebuster). This page explains what data the game handles and who receives it. Questions go to mail@codebuster.de.

Data that stays on your device

The game saves your level progress and star ratings, your settings (sound, haptics, reduced motion, analytics on or off), a session counter used to space out ads, your ad consent result and whether you bought "Remove ads". These are stored locally on the phone, and I have no server that receives them. Uninstalling the app removes them from the phone. If your phone backs up app data (Android backup to your Google account, iCloud or computer backups on iOS), the backup can keep a copy and restore it when you reinstall; that copy is managed by Google or Apple under your account settings, not by me.

Gameplay analytics

The game records events such as starting, finishing or leaving a level, using a hint and watching an ad. An event contains level ids, chapter numbers, durations and counts like hints or undos. It never contains your name, email address, location, device name, account or advertising identifier.

Right now these events are held in memory on the device and are not sent anywhere. If a future version sends them to an analytics service, this policy will name that service before that version ships. You can switch analytics off under Settings at any time; the game then records nothing.

Advertising

Dyed shows ads through Google AdMob. The game does not read your advertising identifier. The Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app does: it can collect the advertising identifier (AAID on Android, IDFA on iOS when you allow tracking), your IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads, so that it can serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Google processes this data under its own terms; see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the EEA, the UK and other places where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before it requests any ad. You can decline personalised ads, and you can change your choice later under Settings, Privacy options. When the form reports that ads may not be requested, the game requests none and stays fully playable. No ad is shown during your first session.

Purchases

"Remove ads" is sold through Google Play or the App Store. The store handles payment; I never see your payment details. The game only learns whether you own the purchase and keeps that on the device.

Children

Dyed is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Your rights

I do not hold personal data about you, so there is nothing for me to export or delete. To remove the game's data, uninstall it and, if you want, delete its copy from your phone's backups. For data the Google Mobile Ads SDK collected, use the controls in your Google account and your phone's ad settings, where you can also reset or delete the advertising identifier. If you are in the EU or UK you can complain to your data protection authority. For anything else, write to mail@codebuster.de.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
