Unplug privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Unplug is an Android app that blocks distracting apps, made by Florian Kirchner (codebuster). This page explains what the app handles and who receives it. Questions go to mail@codebuster.de.

No account, no server

Unplug does not require an account and does not send information to a server operated by me. Settings and progress are stored on the device: the app rules you create, screen-time goals, session and schedule state, and progress such as streaks, XP, quests and badges. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes them.

Accessibility service

Unplug uses Android's Accessibility service to read the package name of the app currently in the foreground. It uses that package name to apply the blocking rules you set and to count app opens and time spent in apps. Unplug does not read on-screen text, does not read what you type, and does not record screen content. The package names, times and counters it derives stay in the app's local storage on your phone.

What Unplug does not do

Unplug contains no advertising SDK and no analytics SDK. It has no sign-in, no cloud sync and no social features. It does not share data with third parties, because it does not send data anywhere.

Device backups and transfers

Depending on your Android backup settings, your phone may copy app data to your Google account or to another device during a transfer. Those copies are controlled by Android and your device settings; I do not operate them and cannot access them. You can manage or delete them in Android's backup settings.

Permissions the app asks for

Notification permission, to show session and scrolling-nudge notifications. Accessibility service access, to see which app is in the foreground. A query for launchable apps, so you can choose which apps your rules apply to. All of these are optional in the sense that they can be switched off in Android settings; blocking does not work without the accessibility service.

Children

Unplug is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. The app holds no personal data about anyone, and no data leaves the device.

Your rights

I do not hold personal data about you, so there is nothing for me to export, correct or delete. To remove the app's local data, clear its storage or uninstall it, and delete any device backup copy through Android's settings. If you are in the EU or UK you can complain to your data protection authority. For anything else, write to mail@codebuster.de.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
